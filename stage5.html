Evaluation Criteria & Severity Scale

Each detected usability issue is documented with its problem title, violated heuristic(s) (Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics), a brief description, a severity rating, actionable suggestions for improvement, and annotated screenshots pinpointing the interface elements to modify.

0 • None

Not considered a usability problem.

1 • Cosmetic

Fix only if extra time is available.

2 • Minor

Low priority usability fix.

3 • Major

Important to fix; high priority.

4 • Catastrophe

Imperative to fix before release.
