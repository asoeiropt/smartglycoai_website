Stage 2: User and task analysis

Objective: Identification and characterization of the target users, tasks and scenarios.

1. Overview

Problem

Managing Type 1 Diabetes requires constant manual interpretation of glucose levels, dietary intake, and physical activity. Existing Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM) systems fail to provide integrated, automated decision support, leaving the cognitive burden of calculating precise insulin doses entirely on the patient.

4. Tasks Analysis

Task 1: Log a meal and get insulin recommendation (Patient)

Objective: Accurately log food intake using computer vision to receive an insulin dose suggestion.
Pre-conditions: App is connected to the CGM sensor and user is logged in.
Time/Freq: 3-5 times a day (before meals), under 1 minute.
Exceptions: Camera fails to recognize food; CGM sensor loses Bluetooth connection.
Sub-tasks:
  • Open the meal logging module.
  • Take a photo of the meal.
  • Confirm or adjust the AI's carbohydrate estimation.
  • Review and accept/reject the suggested insulin dose.

Task 2: Review patient's glycemic history (Doctor/Nurse)

Objective: Analyze weekly/monthly glucose trends to adjust medical treatment.
Pre-conditions: Professional has access to the patient's shared data dashboard.
Time/Freq: Once a month or during clinical appointments.
Exceptions: Patient has not synced their data recently.
Sub-tasks:
  • Access the specific patient's profile.
  • Filter data by the last 30 days.
  • Identify patterns of hypoglycemia or hyperglycemia.

5. Scenarios

Scenario 1 (Patient): Meal Time at a Restaurant

João is at a restaurant and is unsure about the carbohydrate count of his pasta dish. He opens SmartGlycoAI, takes a photo of his plate, and the app estimates 65g of carbs. Based on his current CGM reading of 110 mg/dL, the app suggests a precise insulin dose before he starts eating.

Scenario 2 (Doctor): Routine Clinical Check-up

Dr. Sofia is preparing for a consultation with João. She opens the platform's professional dashboard to review his data from the past month. She notices frequent hypoglycemic events during the night and decides to adjust his baseline insulin ratio directly within the app's professional settings.
