⚕️ Medical Terminology

T1D (Type 1 Diabetes)

A chronic condition in which the pancreas produces little or no insulin. Patients must manually manage their blood glucose levels through external insulin administration, diet, and lifestyle adjustments.

CGM (Continuous Glucose Monitor)

A wearable sensor (e.g., FreeStyle Libre) that automatically tracks glucose levels day and night. SmartGlycoAI connects to these devices via Bluetooth to provide real-time readings and trend graphs.

ICR (Insulin-to-Carb Ratio)

A personalized setting that indicates how many grams of carbohydrates are covered by 1 unit of insulin. For example, an ICR of 1:15 means the patient needs 1 unit of insulin for every 15g of carbs eaten.

ISF (Insulin Sensitivity Factor)

Defines how much 1 unit of fast-acting insulin will lower the patient's blood glucose level. Used by the app to calculate correction doses when glucose is too high.

Hypoglycemia (Low)

A dangerous condition where blood sugar drops below healthy levels (typically < 70 mg/dL). SmartGlycoAI issues predictive alerts to warn users before this happens, suggesting fast-acting carbs (like juice) to correct it.

Hyperglycemia (High)

When blood glucose is too high (typically > 180 mg/dL). Often occurs if a meal was miscalculated or not enough insulin was administered. Repeated highs are flagged in the doctor's dashboard.

Bolus & Basal Insulin

Basal insulin provides a slow, steady background supply throughout the day and night, while Bolus insulin is a fast-acting dose taken before meals or to correct high glucose—which SmartGlycoAI calculates automatically.

Fast-Acting Carbohydrates

Simple sugars (such as fruit juice or glucose tablets) absorbed rapidly into the bloodstream to treat or prevent an impending hypoglycemic episode within 15 to 20 minutes.

HCI & UI Terminology

Paper Prototype (Low-Fidelity)

A non-digital, hand-drawn representation of the user interface (used in Stage 3). It allows designers to quickly test navigation flows and screen layouts with users before writing any code.

Cognitive Load

The amount of mental effort required by a user to complete a task. SmartGlycoAI aims to drastically reduce the cognitive load of diabetic patients by automating carbohydrate estimation and insulin math.

Usability Testing

An evaluation method where users are asked to perform specific tasks (scenarios) using the prototype, while observers note errors, hesitations, and feedback to improve the design.

Edge Case / Fallback

A rare or extreme situation (e.g., losing internet connection or the AI failing to recognize food). The UI must provide a "fallback" option (like manual entry) to ensure the system remains safe and usable.

Wizard of Oz Prototyping

An evaluation technique (used in Stage 4) where a human team member discreetly simulates complex backend intelligence—such as AI food recognition and live CGM alerts—so users can test a realistic system before full implementation.

Prototype Fidelity (Look, Breadth & Depth)

Measures how closely a prototype resembles the final product across visual polish (look & feel), feature coverage (breadth), and functional backend logic (depth).
