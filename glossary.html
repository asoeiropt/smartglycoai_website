Medical & Technical Glossary

Key terminology and clinical concepts foundational to the SmartGlycoAI platform architecture.

CGM (Continuous Glucose Monitor)

A wearable medical device that tracks blood glucose levels continuously in real-time (every few minutes) using a tiny sensor inserted under the skin. In SmartGlycoAI, this data is synced via Bluetooth to feed biometric inputs into our decision support models.

Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus (T1D)

A chronic autoimmune condition where the pancreas produces little or no insulin. Patients require lifelong exogenous insulin administration and rigorous, continuous monitoring of carbohydrate intake and physical activity to maintain safe glycemic ranges.

Hypoglycemia & Hyperglycemia

Hypoglycemia: A dangerous state where blood sugar drops below normal levels (typically < 70 mg/dL), causing dizziness or fainting.
Hyperglycemia: An elevated blood sugar level resulting from insufficient insulin or excess carbohydrate intake. Both extremes are primary risks mitigated by SmartGlycoAI's proactive alerts.

Carbohydrate Counting & Insulin Ratio

Carb Counting: The practice of calculating the grams of carbohydrates in a meal to determine the required insulin bolus.
Insulin-to-Carb Ratio (ICR): A personalized medical metric defining how many grams of carbohydrates are covered by 1 unit of rapid-acting insulin. Our app automates this complex calculation using computer vision and AI.

Basal vs. Bolus Insulin

Basal: Long-acting background insulin that keeps blood sugar stable overnight and between meals.
Bolus: Rapid-acting insulin doses taken specifically to cover meals or correct high blood glucose levels.
