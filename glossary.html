Medical Glossary

Essential terms and concepts used in the SmartGlycoAI ecosystem to help users and evaluators understand Type 1 Diabetes management.

🩸

T1D (Type 1 Diabetes)

A chronic condition in which the pancreas produces little or no insulin. Patients must manually manage their blood glucose levels through external insulin administration, diet, and lifestyle adjustments.

📱

CGM (Continuous Glucose Monitor)

A wearable sensor (e.g., FreeStyle Libre) that automatically tracks glucose levels day and night. SmartGlycoAI connects to these devices via Bluetooth to provide real-time readings and trend graphs.

⚖️

ICR (Insulin-to-Carb Ratio)

A personalized setting that indicates how many grams of carbohydrates are covered by 1 unit of insulin. For example, an ICR of 1:15 means the patient needs 1 unit of insulin for every 15g of carbs eaten.

📉

ISF (Insulin Sensitivity Factor)

Defines how much 1 unit of fast-acting insulin will lower the patient's blood glucose level. Used by the app to calculate correction doses when glucose is too high.

⚠️

Hypoglycemia (Low)

A dangerous condition where blood sugar drops below healthy levels (typically < 70 mg/dL). SmartGlycoAI issues predictive alerts to warn users before this happens, suggesting fast-acting carbs (like juice) to correct it.

📈

Hyperglycemia (High)

When blood glucose is too high (typically > 180 mg/dL). Often occurs if a meal was miscalculated or not enough insulin was administered. Repeated highs are flagged in the doctor's dashboard.

💉

Bolus Insulin

A single, fast-acting dose of insulin given specifically to handle the carbohydrates in a meal or to correct a high glucose reading. This is the main calculation performed by SmartGlycoAI.

🎯

Target Glucose

The ideal blood sugar level the patient aims to maintain (usually around 100-120 mg/dL). The application's AI algorithms calculate corrections to always push the patient's glucose back to this target.
